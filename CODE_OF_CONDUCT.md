# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in the Veyrith project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that contribute to a positive environment include being respectful, accepting good-faith critique, focusing on what is best for the project, and showing empathy toward other contributors. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, publishing private information, or other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this code. Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially where possible.

This code is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
